How the reports were split

  1. One id, one report. The ticket export has overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Duplicates by id are counted once.
  2. By tenant, not by intake tag. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: their locale and their booking form (causes.ts). Every tenant on file writes the day first.
  3. parser: the shared parser reads dates month first, so a day-first tenant’s date comes out with day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). App, email, and forms on booking-v2.
  4. hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type month first, and the form reads the date day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this one.
  5. shift: the reports the desk already flagged as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The date moves by one day, or differs by where the attendee is (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). That is a time-zone bug, not a swap. Every other report names two dates that are an exact day/month swap of each other.

What changes for the desk

  • Matching on subject merged three bugs into one ticket. Subject lines say that a date is wrong, not why.
  • Before merging a big ticket, read a sample of the bodies and group by tenant and channel.
  • New reports land in the ticket of their problem; the table above counts from the data at render time.
